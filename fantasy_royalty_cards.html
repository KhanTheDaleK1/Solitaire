Design Anatomy of this Theme:

  • Parchment Stock: Subtle warm ivory (#f7f2e4) paired with a charcoal-umber border (#4a4233) to ground the fantasy aesthetic without glowing white.
  • Asymmetrical Storybook Characters: Full-bust illustrated portraits with expressive hair dynamics (crimson, teal, dark curls) and historical garments (pauldrons, ermine fur, tiaras).
  • Ornate Filigree Pips: Rather than flat geometric suits, each suit features inner cutouts and heraldic flourish inlays.
  • Maximized Ace of Spades/Clubs: The Ace features an oversized centerpiece heraldic emblem (~60% of card height) with two-tone shaded ornamental detailing.
